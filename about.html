Our Story

We saw real potential being ignored – we fixed it

Kesh Aura was founded in 2023 in Marrakesh — not inside a boardroom, but by a team of friends who understood both creativity and business. We came from different backgrounds, but we shared one belief: every brand with real value deserves to be taken seriously online. That belief became our foundation.

We kept seeing the same pattern across industries — local businesses, personal brands, and entrepreneurs pouring everything into their work, yet showing up online as if they didn't care. No clear strategy, no consistent identity, no real results. Their work spoke for itself offline, but digitally, they were invisible.

Today, we offer everything a brand needs to show up and stand out — from social media management, branding, and web design to paid advertising and content creation. We don't just deliver tasks. We treat every project like it's our own, because that's the only way to get results that actually matter.

Since day one, we've partnered with 37+ clients — local businesses, personal brands, companies, doctors, lawyers, and service providers — each with a different story, a different market, and one shared goal: your results.
