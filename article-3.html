How We Achieved 3× ROI for a Local Business Using Meta Ads

6 min read · January 2025 · Kesh Aura

This is a real case study. A local service business in Morocco — we will keep the name private — came to us after spending three months running Meta ads with zero return. Their budget was gone. Their confidence was gone. They were close to giving up on digital advertising entirely.

Six weeks later, they were getting 3× return on every dirham they spent. Here is exactly what we did.

Step 1: We Stopped the Bleeding

The first thing we did was turn off all their current ads. Every single one. The campaigns were built wrong from the start — wrong objective, wrong audience, wrong creative. Running them longer was burning money.

Sometimes the best thing you can do is stop.

Step 2: We Built the Right Audience

The previous campaigns were targeting broad demographics. Age and location — nothing else. We built audiences based on behaviors: people who had visited their website, people similar to their best customers, people engaging with competitors.

The audience got smaller. The results got bigger.

Step 3: We Fixed the Creative

The old ads looked like ads. People scroll past ads. We created content that looked native to the feed — real situations, real results, real language their customers use. No stock photos. No generic headlines.

The Result

Week one: break even. Week two: 1.8× return. Week four: 3× return consistently. The budget did not change. The thinking did.

That is what a real paid ads strategy looks like.
